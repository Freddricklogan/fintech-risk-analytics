Simulated daily return distribution (Monte Carlo VaR)

Historical VaR: interpolated 5th percentile of daily portfolio returns. Parametric: −(μ + σ·Φ⁻¹(0.05)). Monte Carlo: jointly normal draws from the empirical mean vector and covariance. All three as positive fractions of value; the dollar figure applies them to today's portfolio value.

Projection

Geometric Brownian motion from today's portfolio value; the expected terminal value is V·e^(μ·T) in closed form.
